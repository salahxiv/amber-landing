Last updated: November 2026

Amber works without a user account. This policy describes which data the website amber.devsalah.com and the Amber app for Mac and iPhone process. The app is currently in preparation; the app sections describe the version being prepared for release.

2. Website

The website is delivered via GitHub Pages and the Cloudflare network. [[OWNER: Hosting bestätigen – laut Antwort-Headern vom 12.11.2026 GitHub Pages hinter Cloudflare]] To deliver the pages, these providers technically process your IP address and request data such as the requested page, time and browser.

The website itself sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising scripts. It only stores your language choice (en or de) in your browser's local storage.

3. Waitlist

If you enter your email address in the waitlist form, it is sent to our self-hosted newsletter software Listmonk (listmonk.devsalah.com). We use it only to tell you when Amber launches. You can ask us to remove it at any time by email.

[[OWNER: Hoster und Serverstandort der Listmonk-Instanz, Einwilligungsverfahren (Double-Opt-in ja/nein), Speicherdauer und Abmeldelink in den Mails ergänzen bzw. bestätigen]]

4. Data stored by the app on your device

Amber stores your settings, timer profiles (Mac), break history, statistics and achievements on your device (UserDefaults and SwiftData). Your break history and statistics are not sent to us. Settings are additionally synchronised via your iCloud account (section 7).

5. Usage statistics (Aptabase)

The app contains the Aptabase SDK and sends usage events to Aptabase. They help us understand which features are used. The app sends these events:

  • App launch (platform: Mac or iPhone)
  • Timer started, paused, resumed
  • Break started (length of the work interval), completed (break length), skipped
  • Changed settings: work and break duration, sound on/off and break type, each with the new value
  • Onboarding completed, Pro screen opened, achievement unlocked (which one), Pro purchased (product ID)

With each event the SDK also sends: time, a random session ID that is renewed after one hour of inactivity, operating system and version, app version and build, device model (e.g. MacBookPro18,3) and system language. The app sends no name, no email address, no advertising ID and no device ID of its own. When connecting, your IP address is technically transmitted to the Aptabase server. The app currently has no setting to turn these statistics off.

Provider: Aptabase — aptabase.com/legal/privacy. [[OWNER: Aptabase-Region (EU oder US, ergibt sich aus dem App-Key) und ob der Key im Release-Build gesetzt ist]]

6. Crash reports (Sentry SDK, self-hosted Bugsink)

The app contains the Sentry SDK, which sends reports about crashes and errors to our self-hosted Bugsink server (bugsink.devsalah.com). A report contains technical information such as the stack trace, app and operating system version, device model and a log of the last app events (breadcrumbs). The SDK is configured not to attach personal default data (sendDefaultPii = false); when connecting, your IP address is still technically transmitted to the server. The app currently has no setting to turn crash reports off. [[OWNER: Hoster und Serverstandort des Bugsink-Servers einsetzen]]

7. Apple services and permissions

  • iCloud: Your settings (e.g. work and break duration, break type, sounds, overlay design, work hours) are synchronised between your devices via your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). The calendar setting stays on each device. If you turn on device reminders (Pro), the time of a break start, your device name as set in the system and a random device ID are also stored there so your other device can show a reminder. We have no access to your iCloud data.
  • Purchases: Amber Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We receive no payment data; the app only checks the transaction signed by Apple on your device.
  • Calendar (Pro, optional): With your permission, the app checks on your device whether an event is currently in progress and skips a due break in that case. Calendar data is not transmitted.
  • Apple Health (iPhone, optional): With your permission, the app writes completed breaks as mindful minutes to Apple Health. It does not read any health data.
  • Notifications: Break reminders are local notifications created on your device.
