Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The Blinzel app works without a user account and collects no data: no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no tracking. This policy describes which data the website blinzel.devsalah.com and the Blinzel app for Mac and iPhone process. The app is currently in preparation; the app sections describe version 1.0, which is being prepared for release.

2. Website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered via the Cloudflare network. To deliver the pages, these providers technically process your IP address and request data such as the requested page, time and browser. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in delivering the website reliably and securely. How long the providers keep this data is described in their privacy policies: GitHub, Cloudflare.

The website itself sets no cookies and loads no fonts, analytics or advertising scripts from third parties. It only stores your language choice (en or de) in your browser's local storage. Cloudflare protects the email addresses on these pages from spam bots: it can replace them in the page and add a small script, served from this website's own address, that displays them in your browser.

3. Contact by email

If you write to us by email, we use your email address and your message only to answer your request. Emails to addresses at devsalah.com are received via Cloudflare Email Routing and forwarded to our mailbox. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: our legitimate interest in answering your request. We delete your messages once your request has been dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

4. The app collects no data

Version 1.0 of the Blinzel app collects no data. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking. The app sends no data to us, and we receive no information about how you use it. Data only leaves your device through Apple services that you use with your Apple ID: iCloud to sync your settings (and, with Pro, device reminders) and the App Store for purchases (section 6).

5. Data stored on your device

Blinzel stores your settings, timer profiles (Mac), break history, statistics and achievements on your device (UserDefaults and SwiftData). Your break history and statistics stay on your device and are not sent to us. Settings are additionally synchronised via your iCloud account (section 6).

6. Apple services and permissions

  • iCloud: Your settings (e.g. work and break duration, break type, sounds, overlay design, work hours) are synchronised between your devices via your iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). The calendar setting stays on each device. If you turn on device reminders (Pro), the time of a break start, your device name as set in the system and a random device ID are also stored there so your other device can show a reminder. We have no access to your iCloud data.
  • Purchases: Blinzel Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We receive no payment data; the app only checks the transaction signed by Apple on your device.
  • Calendar (Pro, optional): With your permission, the app checks on your device whether an event is currently in progress and skips a due break in that case. Calendar data is not transmitted.
  • Apple Health (iPhone, optional): With your permission, the app writes completed breaks as mindful minutes to Apple Health. It does not read any health data.
  • Notifications: Break reminders are local notifications created on your device; the app uses no push notifications.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to object. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, write to blinzel@devsalah.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the app, the website or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the current version.
