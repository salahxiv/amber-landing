Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

🔒 Amber is designed with privacy at its core. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data. Your data stays on your device.

Data Collection

Amber does not collect any personally identifiable information. All app data — including timer settings, statistics, and achievements — is stored locally on your device using UserDefaults and SwiftData.

Anonymous Analytics

Amber uses Aptabase, a privacy-first analytics service, to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand which features are used and improve the app.

What is tracked (anonymously):

  • App launches (platform: Mac or iPhone)
  • Timer actions (start, pause, resume)
  • Break completions and skips
  • Achievement unlocks
  • Settings changes (which setting, not personal data)

Aptabase does not use cookies, does not track across apps, and does not collect IP addresses, device IDs, or any personal information. Data is processed in the EU and is fully GDPR compliant.

iCloud Sync

If you use multiple Apple devices, Amber can sync your settings via iCloud (NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore). This is handled entirely by Apple's iCloud infrastructure. We do not operate any servers and have no access to your synced data.

HealthKit (iOS)

On iPhone, Amber can optionally write mindfulness minutes to Apple Health. This requires your explicit permission. Health data is stored locally by Apple's HealthKit framework and is never sent to us or any third party.

Calendar Access

Amber can optionally access your calendar to skip breaks during meetings (Pro feature). Calendar data is read locally using Apple's EventKit and is never transmitted anywhere. This requires your explicit permission.

Purchases

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Third-Party Services

Amber uses the following third-party services:

  • Aptabase — Anonymous analytics (EU-hosted, GDPR compliant) — aptabase.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple iCloud — Settings sync — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Apple StoreKit — In-app purchases

Children's Privacy

Amber does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not require any personal information to function.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
