The 20-20-20 Rule

A simple rule of thumb for screen work — Amber reminds you of it. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 6 meters (20 feet) away for 20 seconds.

20
Minutes of work Focus on your screen — Amber runs quietly.

Built for focus.

Not another annoying popup. Amber adapts to how you work.

Customizable Timer

Set work intervals from 1–60 minutes and rest from 5–120 seconds. Amber runs quietly in your menu bar and shows a fullscreen break overlay on every display when it's time.

Achievements

11 achievements for streaks, discipline, and milestones. Track daily stats.

Calming Breaks

Breathing animation, particle effects, and five break types such as 20-20-20, stretching, walking, and 4-7-8 breathing.

Calendar Aware

Pro: skips a break that falls due while your calendar shows an event in progress. Calendar data stays on your device.

iCloud Sync

Your settings follow you between Mac and iPhone via iCloud. No account needed — just your Apple ID. With Pro, a break on one device can also show a reminder on your other device; the timers themselves are not kept in sync.

macOS

Lives in your menu bar. Zero distractions.

  • Menu bar countdown (Pro)
  • Multi-monitor overlays
  • Idle detection — auto-pause when you leave
  • Optionally skip breaks while a fullscreen app is in front
  • Optionally launch at login

iPhone

Widgets, Live Activities, and more.

  • Live Activity on the Lock Screen & Dynamic Island
  • Home screen timer widget
  • Optional: log completed breaks as mindful minutes in Apple Health
  • Haptic feedback
