The 20-20-20 Rule

A simple rule of thumb for screen work — Blinzel reminds you of it. Every 20 minutes, look at something about 6 meters (20 feet) away for 20 seconds.

Built for focus.

Not another annoying popup. Blinzel adapts to how you work.

Customizable Timer

Set work intervals from 1–60 minutes and rest from 5–120 seconds. Blinzel runs quietly in your menu bar and shows a fullscreen break overlay on every display when it's time.

Statistics & Achievements

Breaks today and over the last 7 days, daily average, streaks and 11 achievements.

Calm Breaks

A calm full-screen break with a breathing animation. On the Mac, choose a break type: 20-20-20, stretch, walk, 4-7-8 breathing or your own.

Calendar Aware

Pro: skips a break that falls due while your calendar shows an event in progress. Calendar data stays on your device.

Work Hours

Reminders only on the days and at the times you choose. Included in the free version.

iCloud Sync

Your settings follow you between Mac and iPhone via iCloud. No account needed — just your Apple ID. With Pro, a break on one device can also show a notification on your other devices with the same iCloud account, while Blinzel is open there. The timers run independently on each device.

macOS

A menu bar app without a Dock icon.

  • Menu bar countdown (Pro)
  • The break appears on every connected display
  • Optionally pause the timer while you're away from your Mac
  • Optionally skip breaks while a fullscreen app is in front
  • Optionally launch at login

iPhone

Notifications, a Live Activity and a Home Screen widget.

  • Break reminders as notifications, even when the app is in the background
  • Live Activity on the Lock Screen & Dynamic Island
  • Timer widget for the Home Screen
  • Optional: log completed breaks as mindful minutes in Apple Health
